# Lanternfall — Official Rulebook

_Edition 2.1 — for 2 to 5 players, ages 10 and up_

Lanternfall is a game of night markets and borrowed light. Each player runs a
lantern stall in the harbor town of Mirewick and tries to be the first to
gather 12 Glow Points before the last tide bell rings.

## 1. Setup

Shuffle the Market deck and deal each player 5 cards. Place the Tide token on
the first space of the Tide Track. Each player starts with 3 Wick tokens and
no Glow Points. The youngest player takes the first turn, and play passes
clockwise.

## 2. Turn structure

A turn has three phases, always in this order: Draw, Trade, then Light.

In the Draw phase you take 2 cards from the Market deck. If the deck is empty,
shuffle the discard pile to form a new one. In the Trade phase you may make
any number of trades with other players, but you may only trade cards, never
Wick tokens or Glow Points. In the Light phase you may play lanterns from your
hand to your stall.

## 3. Lanterns and Glow Points

A lantern costs Wick tokens equal to the number printed in its top-left corner.
Each lantern in your stall scores the Glow Points printed at the bottom of the
card. A stall may hold at most 6 lanterns; to play a seventh, you must first
discard one of your own lanterns.

Fog cards are the only cards that can be played outside your own Light phase.
When a Fog card is played, the targeted lantern produces no Glow Points until
the end of that round.

## 4. The Tide

At the end of every round, move the Tide token forward one space. When the Tide
token reaches the last space, the tide bell rings and the game ends at the end
of that round, even if nobody has reached 12 Glow Points.

## 5. Hand limit

At the end of your turn, you may hold no more than 7 cards in hand. Discard
down to 7 if you have more. Wick tokens and lanterns in your stall do not count
toward the hand limit.

## 6. Winning and ties

The first player to reach 12 Glow Points wins immediately. If the tide bell
rings first, the player with the most Glow Points wins. If players are tied,
the tied player with the most unspent Wick tokens wins. If they are still tied,
they share the victory.

## 7. Two-player variant

With exactly two players, remove all Fog cards from the Market deck before
dealing, and each player starts with 4 Wick tokens instead of 3.

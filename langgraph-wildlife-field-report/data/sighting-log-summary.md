# Sighting log summary (all sectors, dry season 2025)

## Elephants
Total logged elephant sightings: 212 across the season, up from 168 last dry season. Largest single herd: 31 individuals at Sablewood Pan in week 6. Herd composition shows 14 calves under two years, the most since monitoring began.

## Big cats
Lion sightings: 58, mostly the Ridge pride and a coalition of three males. Leopard sightings: 9, all at dusk or by camera trap. No cheetah were recorded this season.

## Antelope and grazers
Impala dominated the grazer counts with roughly 1,900 recorded individuals. Zebra numbers held steady near 410. Buffalo herds averaged 55 animals and concentrated on the Ironbark floodplain after the pans dried.

## Wild dogs
Wild dog sightings: 27, nearly all linked to the Mopane Gully den. This is a conservation priority species, with an estimated national population below 700 animals.

## Rare and notable
One pangolin was recorded by camera trap in week 10, the second reserve record. A pair of southern ground hornbills nested in a baobab near the old airstrip.

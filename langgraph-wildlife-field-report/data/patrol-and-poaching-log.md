# Patrol and poaching log (dry season 2025)

## Patrol effort
Rangers completed 148 foot patrols and 41 vehicle patrols this season, covering about 3,900 km. Patrol effort was 12 percent higher than last dry season after two new rangers joined in week 6.

## Snares and traps
Total snares removed: 71, compared with 94 last season. Most were found in the south near Mopane Gully and along the northern fence line. Eleven animals were found with snare injuries; 8 were treated and released and 3 died.

## Arrests and incidents
Four people were arrested for snaring and one for carrying a rifle inside the boundary. No elephant, rhino or lion poaching was recorded. One porcupine and two duiker were found dead in snares.

## Camera traps
18 camera traps ran all season and captured 9,400 usable images. The cameras caught two night-time snare-line walkers near the southern fence, which led to two of the arrests. A leopard was photographed 6 times at Kite Rock.

## Staffing and gaps
The reserve has 14 rangers for roughly 620 square kilometres. Rangers flagged the lack of a second patrol vehicle as the main limit on southern coverage, and asked for radio batteries to be replaced before the next season.

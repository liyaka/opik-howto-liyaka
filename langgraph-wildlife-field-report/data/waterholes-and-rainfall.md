# Waterholes and rainfall (dry season 2025)

## Rainfall record
Rainfall for the 2024-25 wet season totalled 410 mm, about 20 percent below the 10-year average of 515 mm. The last significant rain fell in early April. Ranger gauges at the north and south stations agreed within 15 mm.

## Waterhole status
Of the reserve's 12 natural pans, 8 had dried up by the end of week 10. Sablewood Pan, Ironbark Pool and the Kite Rock seep held water the longest. Ironbark Pool never dried but fell to about 20 percent of its wet-season volume.

## Trucked water
Rangers trucked 31,000 litres to troughs over the season, concentrated in weeks 12 to 16. Most trucked water went to the secondary trough north of Sablewood Pan.

## Borehole plan
A site survey in week 15 marked a borehole location 800 metres from Sablewood Pan. Estimated cost is 18,500 in local grant funds. Drilling would need approval from the reserve board before the next dry season.

# Ranger notes: South Sector (Kestrel Hollow Reserve, dry season 2025)

## Week 2, Ironbark floodplain
Ranger Tendai Mbeki counted 62 buffalo and 31 zebra on the Ironbark floodplain at first light. The grass was short and heavily grazed. Two lion prides were heard calling from the ridge to the east.

## Week 4, wild dog den
A pack of African wild dogs, 9 adults and 7 pups, denned in an old aardvark burrow near Mopane Gully. Ranger Mbeki set a 300 metre no-vehicle buffer around the den. This is the first wild dog denning recorded in the South Sector since 2021.

## Week 7, lion pride movements
The Ridge pride (5 lionesses, 6 cubs) killed a buffalo near Ironbark on the 2nd and fed for two days. Vultures numbering about 40 gathered at the carcass. Ranger notes record no conflict with tourist vehicles.

## Week 9, snare sweep
A three-day sweep of the Mopane Gully drainage found 23 wire snares, 19 of them set along game trails near the reserve's southern edge. One zebra with a snare wound was darted and treated by the vet team and released.

## Week 12, giraffe count
Ranger Mbeki counted 17 giraffe in the South Sector, including 3 calves under six months old. The calves are the first recorded in two seasons.

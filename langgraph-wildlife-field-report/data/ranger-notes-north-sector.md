# Ranger notes: North Sector (Kestrel Hollow Reserve, dry season 2025)

## Week 3, Sablewood Pan
Ranger Imani Okoro logged 14 elephants at Sablewood Pan on the 19th, including a matriarch with a collar that stopped transmitting in May. The pan was down to a third of its wet-season width. Two calves showed visible dust-bathing stress. No conflict with the nearby grazing lease this week.

## Week 5, fence line check
Fence posts 41 to 58 along the northern boundary were bent inward by elephants pushing for the Marula grove outside the reserve. Ranger Okoro's team repaired 11 posts in two days. Crop-raiding reports from the village of Tumbi dropped from six to two after the repair.

## Week 8, Marula grove
Marula fruit set was heavy this year. Elephant visits to the grove inside the reserve boundary rose to nine in one week, the highest recorded. Ranger notes say herds are staying inside the reserve for the fruit instead of crossing the fence line.

## Week 11, leopard sign
Fresh leopard tracks and one scat sample near Kite Rock. Ranger Okoro believes this is the young male first photographed in March. Impala alarm calls were heard at dusk on three evenings.

## Week 14, water pressure
Sablewood Pan fell to a puddle by the 6th. The ranger team trucked 4,000 litres to the secondary trough, and elephants used it within the hour. Rangers recommend drilling the planned borehole before the next dry season.

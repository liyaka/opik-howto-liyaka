# Veldara System Handbook (fictional reference)

[H-01] Veldara is a binary star system 41 light-years from Earth, in the constellation Lyra Minor. The primary, Veldara A, is an orange K-type dwarf of 0.82 solar masses. Its companion, Veldara B, is a red dwarf of 0.31 solar masses.

[H-02] The two stars orbit their common centre of mass once every 212 years, at an average separation of 38 astronomical units.

[H-03] Veldara A has four known planets. Tessen, the innermost, orbits at 0.21 AU and completes a year in 18 days.

[H-04] Maret, the second planet, is a rocky world 1.3 times the diameter of Earth. It orbits at 0.58 AU with a 142-day year and is the only planet in the habitable zone. Spectroscopy shows water vapour and nitrogen in its atmosphere; no oxygen has been detected.

[H-05] Orrin, the third planet, is a gas giant of 1.1 Jupiter masses at 2.4 AU. It has nine known moons.

[H-06] Calyx, the outermost planet, is an ice giant at 11 AU. Its ring system was found in 2031 when it passed in front of a background star.

[H-07] Orrin's largest moon, Piper, is 3,900 km across. Its nitrogen atmosphere has a surface pressure of 1.6 bar, higher than Earth's.

[H-08] The Halden Space Telescope's transit survey found Tessen in 2019. Maret followed in 2021, after a longer observing campaign.

[H-09] Veldara B has no known planets. A 2027 survey ruled out any planet larger than Neptune within 3 AU of it.

[H-10] Veldara A is 6.1 billion years old, about 1.5 billion years older than the Sun.

[H-11] Veldara A shines at 0.38 of the Sun's luminosity, with a surface temperature of 4,900 K.

[H-12] Maret is not tidally locked. It rotates once every 31 hours, so it has a day and a night.

[H-13] Tessen is scorched: its dayside averages 640 K.

[H-14] A flyby of Orrin, the Meridian probe, was proposed for 2040. No spacecraft has ever been launched toward the Veldara system.

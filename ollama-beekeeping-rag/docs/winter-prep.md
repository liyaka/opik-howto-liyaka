# Winter Preparation — Larkspur Hollow Apiary Handbook

## Feeding
If a hive weighs less than 25 kg in the middle of October, feed 2:1 sugar syrup (two parts sugar to one part water by weight) until it reaches that weight. Stop all liquid feeding by the first of November, because bees cannot dry the syrup in cold weather.

## Reducing the entrance
Fit a mouse guard with 9 mm holes on every hive by the 20th of October. Mice move in once nights drop below 5 C and will destroy comb.

## Ventilation and wrapping
Tilt each hive slightly forward so condensation runs out of the front. Leave the top vent open and wrap only the sides with insulation board. A wet cluster dies faster than a cold one.

## Winter checks
Do not open the hive between November and the first week of March. Instead, lift the back of the hive on the 1st of each month to judge weight, and listen at the side for a hum.

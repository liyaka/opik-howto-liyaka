# Varroa Treatment Calendar — Larkspur Hollow Apiary Handbook

## Monitoring
Run an alcohol wash on 300 bees (about half a cup) from a brood frame every month from April to October. The treatment threshold is three mites per 100 bees. Record the count on the hive card.

## Late-summer treatment
The main varroa treatment is applied right after the last honey is removed, usually in the first week of August. Use thymol strips for six weeks, only when daytime highs are below 30 C. If highs exceed 30 C, use formic acid pads for seven days instead.

## Winter oxalic dribble
In the broodless window, normally the second week of December, give one oxalic acid dribble of 5 ml per seam of bees. Do not dribble more than once per winter, and never when the temperature is below 3 C.

## Never treat with supers on
No varroa treatment strip or pad may stay in a hive while honey supers are on, because the residue taints the honey.

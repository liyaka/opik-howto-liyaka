# Honey Harvest — Larkspur Hollow Apiary Handbook

## When to harvest
Take honey only from frames that are at least 90 percent capped. Uncapped honey above 18.5 percent moisture will ferment in the jar. Check moisture with a refractometer on three frames per super before pulling it.

## Clearing the bees
Place a clearer board under the super the evening before. By morning at most a handful of bees remain. Never use a blower on weekends when the neighbouring school garden is in use.

## Extracting
Warm the supers to 30 C for twelve hours so the honey flows. Uncap with a heated knife, spin in the extractor at low speed for five minutes, then increase for five more. Strain through a double sieve of 600 and 200 micron mesh.

## Leaving enough
Always leave the colony at least 20 kg of honey for winter. A full deep frame holds about 3 kg, so that is roughly seven full frames.

# Hive Inspections — Larkspur Hollow Apiary Handbook

## Inspection rhythm
From the first warm week of spring until the main honey flow, open every hive once every nine days. Inspect on a calm day above 14 C, between 11:00 and 15:00, when most foragers are out. After the flow starts, drop to once every fourteen days so the colony is not chilled or robbed.

## What to look for
Check for eggs, which prove the queen was laying in the last three days. Look for capped brood in a compact pattern, and for at least one frame of stored pollen. A spotty brood pattern with more than one empty cell in five means the queen is failing and must be replaced.

## Keeping the colony calm
Use two puffs of smoke at the entrance, wait one minute, then lift the lid. Never inspect on the day a hive was last treated with oxalic acid. Return every frame to its original position and orientation.

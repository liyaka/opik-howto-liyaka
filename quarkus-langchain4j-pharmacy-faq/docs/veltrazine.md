# Veltrazine 10 mg tablets (fictional)

Veltrazine is a once-daily antihistamine for seasonal hay fever and itchy hives.

## Dosing
Adults and children over 12: one 10 mg tablet once a day, with or without food. Do not take more than one tablet in 24 hours. Children aged 6 to 12: half a tablet once a day. Not for children under 6.

## Interactions
Alcohol can make drowsiness stronger. Do not combine with Dormaxin or other sedating sleep aids. Tell your pharmacist if you take any medicine for epilepsy.

## Storage
Keep below 25 C in the original blister pack, away from damp. Tablets expire 36 months after manufacture.

## Side effects
Common: mild drowsiness, dry mouth, headache. Rare: fast heartbeat or swelling of the face. Stop taking it and seek help straight away if you notice swelling of the face or throat.

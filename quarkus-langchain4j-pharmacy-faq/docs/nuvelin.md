# Nuvelin 200 mg film-coated tablets (fictional)

Nuvelin is an over-the-counter anti-inflammatory painkiller for headache, period pain and muscle aches.

## Dosing
Adults: one or two 200 mg tablets every 8 hours with food or milk. Maximum 6 tablets (1200 mg) in 24 hours. Do not use for more than 5 days for pain without talking to a pharmacist or doctor. Not recommended under 14 years.

## Interactions
Do not take with Corbexol or other blood pressure medicines without checking first. Do not combine with other anti-inflammatory painkillers. Avoid alcohol, which increases the risk of stomach irritation. Tell your pharmacist if you take a blood thinner.

## Storage
Store below 25 C, out of direct sunlight and out of reach of children. The blister strip keeps tablets safe for 24 months.

## Side effects
Common: indigestion, nausea, mild stomach pain. Rare: black stools or vomiting blood. Stop taking it and get medical help at once if you see black stools.

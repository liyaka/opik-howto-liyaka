# Dormaxin 25 mg chewable tablets (fictional)

Dormaxin is a short-term sleep aid for occasional trouble falling asleep.

## Dosing
Adults: one chewable tablet 30 minutes before bed, no more than once per night. Use for no more than 7 nights in a row. Not for anyone under 18. Do not drive or operate machinery the morning after taking it if you still feel drowsy.

## Interactions
Do not combine with Veltrazine, alcohol or any other medicine that makes you sleepy. Some cold remedies contain similar ingredients, so check the label.

## Storage
Keep in a cool dry place below 25 C. Once a strip is opened, use the tablets within 6 months.

## Side effects
Common: morning grogginess, dry mouth, blurred vision. Rare: confusion or agitation, especially in older adults. Speak to your pharmacist if grogginess lasts beyond mid-morning.

# Glycavora 500 mg tablets (fictional)

Glycavora is a prescription medicine that helps control blood sugar in adults with type 2 diabetes.

## Dosing
Start with one 500 mg tablet with the evening meal. After one week your prescriber may add a second tablet with breakfast. Always take it with food, which reduces stomach upset. The maximum is four tablets a day.

## Interactions
Alcohol raises the risk of low blood sugar and a rare build-up of acid in the blood, so keep drinking to a minimum. Tell your pharmacist before any scan that uses contrast dye. Some water tablets can change how Glycavora works.

## Storage
Store below 25 C in the original container. Do not store in the bathroom. Insulin or injectable pens are not stored with these tablets; ask your pharmacist how to keep those.

## Side effects
Common: nausea, loose stools, a metallic taste in the first weeks. Rare: unusual muscle pain with fast breathing. Seek urgent help if you feel very tired with fast breathing and muscle pain.

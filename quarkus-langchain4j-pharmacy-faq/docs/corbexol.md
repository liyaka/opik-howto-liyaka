# Corbexol 5 mg and 10 mg tablets (fictional)

Corbexol is a prescription medicine that lowers high blood pressure.

## Dosing
Usual adult starting dose is 5 mg once a day in the morning. Your prescriber may raise it to 10 mg after four weeks. If you miss a dose, take it as soon as you remember unless it is nearly time for the next one; never take a double dose.

## Interactions
Grapefruit juice raises the level of Corbexol in the blood, so avoid it. Anti-inflammatory painkillers such as Nuvelin can weaken its effect and strain the kidneys. Potassium supplements and salt substitutes should only be used on your prescriber's advice.

## Storage
Store at room temperature, below 30 C. Keep the bottle tightly closed. Discard 90 days after first opening.

## Side effects
Common: dizziness when standing up, ankle swelling, a dry cough. Rare: fainting. Contact your prescriber if the dizziness does not settle after the first two weeks.
